This readme explains how to use the application.

From the main screen, the workout tab, the user may start a new workout by tapping "Start Workout"

After a workout is started, a timer immediately begins counting up. 

The user may end the workout by tapping "End Workout" at any time.

To start an exercise, the user must be in an active workout. The user may then tap on an exercise and the exercise will begin.

The user may end the exercise by tapping "End Exercise" at any time.

To add a new exercise, the user may tap "Add Exercise" on the workout tab. The user will be prompted to enter the name, description, type, and add an image for the exercise. 
The user may then tap "Add Exercise" to add the exercise to the list of exercises.
These exercises will be saved between sessions.

After the user has completed their workout, they will be directed to the History tab. Here, the user may view their workout history. 
You can also navigate to the History tab by tapping the "History" tab at the bottom of the screen at any time, even during an active workout.
The user may tap on a workout on the History tab to view information about what exercises were completed during that workout.
The user may delete items from the history by swiping left on the item and tapping "Delete".
Alternatively, they may clear the entire history by tapping "Clear History" at the top of the screen.

On the goals tab, also accessible at any time by tapping the "Goals" tab at the bottom of the screen, the user may view their goals.
When a user has completed a workout during a day, the user will have a dot by the day of the week on the goals tab.
The user may decide how many days they would like to workout each week by tapping "Change Goal" and picking a number.
